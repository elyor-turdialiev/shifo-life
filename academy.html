Qaysi darajadan boshlashim kerak?С какого уровня мне начинать?What level do I need to start?

Tariflar bir-biridan nimasi bilan farq qiladi?Чем отличаются тарифы?What’s the difference between the plans?

Medical English follows the Shifo Life medical course. Intense adds more speaking club, more lessons and a second mentoring session.
